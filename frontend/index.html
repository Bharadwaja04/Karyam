say it messy.

Tap the mic and talk through your day in any mix of languages. Karyam sorts it into projects, tasks and dates, and asks when it isn't sure.

Get set up (about a minute)

  1. Connect a language model. This is what lets Karyam understand you. Typing works as soon as this is done.
  2. Add your Gnani key. This adds your voice (talking and being spoken to).
Try saying

Ready when you are. Tap the mic (or press Space) and talk.

  1. 1 Say or type it
  2. 2 Karyam files it into projects and dates
  3. 3 It reminds you on time
